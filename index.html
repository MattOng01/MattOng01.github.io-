Kansas City House Price Appreciation
→
Spatial Econometrics Real Estate Trends Housing Markets Data Visualization

Analyzed housing market convergence across 198 Kansas City ZIP codes using Zillow Home Value Index (ZHVI) data, GIS, and spatial econometric models. Found that lower-priced neighborhoods consistently appreciated faster than higher-priced neighborhoods, and that this relationship remained significant after accounting for geographic clustering. The project combines interactive mapping, data visualization, and econometric analysis to examine long-run neighborhood change.

GDP Growth and Racial Unemployment Gaps Across U.S. Metro Areas
→
Labor Economics Applied Economics SAS

Analyzed economic growth and labor market equity across U.S. metropolitan areas from 2010–2023. Developed a performance metric that rewarded stable GDP growth while penalizing volatility, then compared racial unemployment gaps between high- and low-performing metros. Found that stronger economic performance did not consistently translate into more equitable employment outcomes, highlighting the limits of growth-focused development strategies.
